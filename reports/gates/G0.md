## 6. Criterion 5: Triton cache archived: **PASS, with a staleness finding**

- **Archive:** `runs/_artifacts/triton_cache.tar` is a deterministic tar. `sha256sum` in this step gives `f417e487…96e3de`, equal to `triton_cache.tar.sha256` and to the registry note (`experiments/registry.jsonl`, `run_id: S0-triton-cache`, trainer-style cache hash `be0c80b1…f74316`). It covers D384/D512 × {C0, C3, C12, X-gc3, X-E3, O-gla3, O-nc3}: training at 2 × 4K and 1 × 32K, 60 eval cells, and benchmark shapes.
- **Coverage at S0:** the 15 G0 production runs, the VT-13 resumes and the production test compiled nothing new (`find -newer` → 0 files at the time).
- **Finding (new in this step).** `find .cache/triton -type f -newer runs/_artifacts/triton_cache.tar | wc -l` → **3,205 files** (396 kernels), dated 2026-11-10 06h (185) and 08h (211).
  - Kernels: `causal_conv1d_{fwd,bwd}` (184), `l2norm` (75), GDN chunk kernels, fla `layer_norm_gated` and `gdn_gate`.
  - These times match the reproduction-programme jobs (VT-21's conv grid at many widths, VT-22's fla LM, R5's d64 Zoology GDN), which share the repo `TRITON_CACHE_DIR`.
  - Effects: the live cache, and hence every future per-checkpoint `triton_cache_hash`, no longer equals the archived hash. The archive also lives under the gitignored `runs/`.
  - It does not invalidate any S0 measurement. This step's full suite and supplementary runs (12:35–13:06) compiled **0** new kernels (`find … -newer triton_cache.tar -newermt '2026-11-10 12:30'` → 0), so the archived shapes still cover every S0 test workload.
  - **Proposed (D056):** re-warm and re-archive at G5 and freeze that SHA. Copy the tarball somewhere durable. Give reproduction and diagnostic jobs a separate `TRITON_CACHE_DIR`.
- **Repo hygiene:** `.cache/` is now gitignored and untracked (`git ls-files .cache | wc -l` → 0).
