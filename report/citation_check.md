# Citation check for `refs.bib`

Checked 2026-11-14. 42 entries were written and none were dropped.

## Method

- **Titles, author lists and primary categories.** Every arXiv entry was generated by script from one arXiv API query (`export.arxiv.org/api/query?id_list=...`, all 39 ids), so the metadata is copied, not retyped. Each returned title matched the paper named in the brief. Author lists are cut to the first 10 names, followed by `and others`. Accented characters are LaTeX-escaped and compound surnames are braced (`{Ben allal}`, `{Van keirsbilck}`).
- **Venues.** Taken from the arXiv comment field where the authors state one. Otherwise from the OpenReview API (`venue`/`venueid` of the accepted note) or the Semantic Scholar batch API. The source for each is listed below. Every peer-reviewed entry keeps `eprint`/`archivePrefix`/`primaryClass`.
- **Page numbers.** Not included for conference papers, because they were not checked against the proceedings. The two journal entries carry volume and pages taken from Semantic Scholar's journal field.

## Claim checks

### 1. Wang et al. 2507.06457, hybrid ratio vs. RULER recall — WRONG AS WORDED (the numbers exist, but they are not what the claim says)

Source: Appendix E, **Table 7** ("Recall performance results from each linear attention variant and ratio are averaged"). The table has columns DeltaNet / GatedDeltaNet / GLA / HGRN2 / **Average**.

| Ratio | DeltaNet | GatedDeltaNet | GLA | HGRN2 | Average |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 24:1 | 0.417 | 0.341 | 0.293 | 0.303 | 0.338 |
| 12:1 | 0.352 | 0.352 | 0.288 | 0.368 | 0.340 |
| 6:1 | 0.371 | 0.425 | 0.330 | 0.434 | 0.390 |
| 3:1 | 0.426 | 0.436 | 0.332 | 0.395 | 0.397 |
| Pure | 0.320 | 0.356 | 0.144 | 0.203 | 0.256 |

- The sequence **0.256 → 0.338 → 0.390 → 0.397 is the "Average" column**, the mean over four mechanisms (DeltaNet, GatedDeltaNet, GLA, HGRN2). It is **not** the GDN sweep.
- **The GDN-only sequence is 0.356 (pure) → 0.341 (24:1) → 0.352 (12:1) → 0.425 (6:1) → 0.436 (3:1).** So 0.356 is pure GatedDeltaNet and 0.256 is the pure-linear average across mechanisms. For GDN, the 24:1 hybrid is slightly *below* the pure model.
- **Model scale.** The Table 7 caption does not state it. Table 2's caption says 340M models were omitted from recall benchmarks because their recall is insignificant, which implies Table 7 is 1.3B, but the paper never says so explicitly.
- **LM metrics.** Appendix E, **Table 6**. Per-ratio "Average" LM scores are 0.476 (24:1), 0.478 (12:1), 0.480 (6:1), 0.479 (3:1) and 0.472 (pure). GDN alone scores 0.468 / 0.478 / 0.481 / 0.478 / 0.487. **The caption says these are "averaged across 1.3B and 340M parameter scales"**, so they are not 1.3B-only. 1.3B-only LM results appear in §4.1, Table 3 (e.g. GatedDeltaNet 24-1 averages 0.565, Transformer 0.548), and §4.2 describes 1.3B LM scores as clustering "around 0.55–0.57".
- **Related numbers.** Appendix C, Table 5 lists GatedDeltaNet-hybrid-3-1 with a RULER average of 0.440 and the Transformer at 0.423. That differs slightly from the 0.436 in Table 7. §4.2 gives a full-attention baseline of "approximately 0.42".
- **Correct phrasing.** "Averaged over four linear-attention families, RULER recall rises 0.256 (pure) → 0.338 (24:1) → 0.390 (6:1) → 0.397 (3:1). For Gated DeltaNet specifically it goes 0.356 → 0.341 → 0.425 → 0.436. LM accuracy averaged over the 340M and 1.3B scales stays at 0.47–0.48 (Tables 6–7)."

### 2. Nemotron-H 2504.03624, ~8% attention layers motivated by Waleffe et al. — VERIFIED

- **§2.1 "Model Architecture".** "As suggested by prior work (Waleffe et al., 2024), we set the number of attention layers to be roughly 8% of the total number of layers and evenly disperse them throughout the model. This amounts to 4 self-attention layers (out of 52 layers) for Nemotron-H-8B and 10 for Nemotron-H-56B (out of 118 layers). The rest of the layers consist of an even split between FFN and Mamba-2 layers."
- **Figure 2 caption.** "Roughly 8% of the total layers in the model are self-attention layers."
- Exact fractions are 4/52 = 7.7% and 10/118 = 8.5%.

### 3. Waleffe et al. 2406.07887, 8B Mamba-2-Hybrid layer counts — VERIFIED

- **§4.2 "Mamba-2-Hybrid 8B"** (Table 6 summarises the architecture). "Out of 56 total layers, the hybrid model has 4 (7.1%) self-attention layers, 24 (42.9%) Mamba-2 layers, and 28 (50%) MLP layers." The Abstract rounds this to "43% Mamba-2, 7% self-attention, and 50% MLP layers".
- **On the attention fraction, §4.1 "Designing a Hybrid SSM-Transformer Model", Figure 4.** In 130M-parameter, 24-layer hybrids, "validation loss is minimized when roughly 8% of the layers are self-attention layers". The 840M experiments "confirm that these findings scale across model sizes", and the result is "consistent with those reported by Dao and Gu 2024".
- **Same section, MLP fraction.** With attention fixed at 8%, "30%–50% of the layers can be MLPs without increasing model loss". 50% MLP trains 20% faster than 5% MLP.

### 4. Nemotron-Flash 2511.18890, full attention → SWA and 32K NIAH — VERIFIED (numbers below)

- **Setup, §4.3 "Ablation on Attention Configuration".** Starting from Nemotron-Flash-3B with 3 full-attention (FA) layers, the authors continue pretraining at 29k context for 25B tokens in three configurations: 3FA, 2FA+1SWA and 1FA+2SWA. All SWA layers use an 8k window.
- **Figure 9** (RULER NIAH heatmap). Scores at **32768** context for niah_single_1 / niah_single_2 / niah_single_3:

  | Configuration | single_1 | single_2 | single_3 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 3FA | 0.96 | 0.91 | 0.69 |
  | 2FA+1SWA | 0.94 | 0.85 | 0.74 |
  | 1FA+2SWA | 0.78 | 0.60 | 0.32 |

  At 24576 context, 1FA+2SWA already falls to 0.98 / 0.73 / 0.59, while 3FA stays at 1.00 / 0.99 / 0.93.
- **Table 8.** Throughput is 2939 tok/s for 3FA, 3737 for 2FA+1SWA and 4657 for 1FA+2SWA ("1.6× higher"). Recall scores are 73.25 / 73.37 / 73.77 and averages are 60.98 / 60.69 / 60.84.
- **Text.** NIAH "drops significantly at longer context lengths when the number of FA layers is reduced to one ... we recommend maintaining at least two full attention layers". Replacing one of three FA layers barely changes 32K NIAH (one sub-task even rises: 0.69 → 0.74). The large drop comes from going down to one FA layer.

### 5. Gated DeltaNet 2412.06464, recurrence — VERIFIED (exact form)

- **§3.1 "Formulation: Gated Delta Rule", Eq. (10):** S_t = S_{t−1} ( α_t ( I − β_t k_t k_tᵀ ) ) + β_t v_t k_tᵀ, where α_t ∈ (0,1) is a data-dependent gating term and β_t ∈ (0,1) is the writing strength (defined in §2.2).
- **Convention: column vectors.** §2.1 defines S_t = S_{t−1} + v_t k_tᵀ ∈ R^{d_v×d_k} with read-out o_t = S_t q_t ∈ R^{d_v}. So q_t, k_t ∈ R^{d_k} and v_t ∈ R^{d_v} are column vectors, the state is d_v × d_k, and the transition matrix multiplies the state from the right.
- **Related forms in the paper.** The delta rule appears in §2.2 as S_t = S_{t−1}(I − β_t k_t k_tᵀ) + β_t v_t k_tᵀ, and Mamba2 as S_t = α_t S_{t−1} + v_t k_tᵀ. The claim's formula matches Eq. (10) exactly.

### 7. Zoology 2312.04927, MQAR depends on dimension for gated-conv/recurrent models; attention solves it at small width — VERIFIED (with one scoping detail)

- **§4.3 "Empirical analysis of gated convolution capacity and associative recall", Claim 1 and Figure 2 (top).** "Gated-convolution models with two layers require model dimension to scale at least linearly in sequence length in order to solve associative recall, while attention models can solve it with near-constant dimensionality ... attention solves Mqar perfectly at all sequence lengths using a constant model dimension of 64. In contrast, Mqar does not achieve accuracy > 0.9 unless d ≥ N." The setup uses vocabulary 8,192, model dimension and sequence length swept from 64 to 512, and two-layer models.
- **Gated recurrences: Appendix F, Experiment 2 and Figure 6.** "Gated recurrent models use larger dimensionality than attention to solve the task as the number of unique key-value pairs to store ... increases". This was tested on RetNet with N=256 and d ∈ {64, 128, 256}. Appendix H.6 shows that the gated recurrence uses Ω(N) bits to solve MQAR.
- **Scoping detail.** For gated convolutions the paper ties required width to **sequence length** (number of token-interaction distances). For gated recurrences it ties width to the **number of key-value pairs**. The headline experiment (Fig. 2) covers attention, BaseConv, RWKV, H3 and Hyena. The recurrent (RetNet) evidence is in the appendix.
